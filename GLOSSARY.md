# Poker Timer

A blind clock for a home group's in-person Texas Hold'em tournaments, which also honours the group's winners.

## Language

### Play

**Tournament**:
One game of poker played in a single sitting, made up of many Hands, that ends when one player holds all the chips.
_Avoid_: Game (when meaning the whole sitting), session, match

**Hand**:
One deal of the cards, from posting the blinds to awarding that hand's chips; a Tournament is a sequence of Hands.
_Avoid_: Round, game, deal

**Betting Round**:
One of the four rounds of betting within a Hand: pre-flop, flop, turn and river.
_Avoid_: Round (unqualified), street

### Outcome

**Winner**:
The one player who ends the Tournament holding all the chips; there are no other placements.
_Avoid_: Champion (when meaning any past winner), first place

**Pot**:
The whole-dollar total the Winner takes; winner takes all.
_Avoid_: Prize, purse, payout

**Reigning Champion**:
The Winner of the most recently played Tournament, honoured on the main screen until the next Tournament is recorded.
_Avoid_: Leader, last winner, defending champ

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Round" has been used for a blind period, a Hand and a Betting Round. It is retired as a bare word; use **Hand** or **Betting Round**.
